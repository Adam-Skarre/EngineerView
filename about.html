Engineer View is a research publication that examines the physical and digital systems beneath markets—from power and infrastructure to industrial capacity and artificial intelligence.

Research mandate

Engineer View’s primary research territory is the infrastructure behind the AI economy: power availability, grid equipment, data-center delivery, and the industrial capacity required to build it.

We follow adjacent questions only when they materially affect that system.

Our research standard ↗

Who it serves

Research is written for investors, executives, developers, utilities, operators, and technical teams making capital-allocation and delivery decisions.

Engineer View is published by Skar Technologies, an engineering and technology company based in New York.
