Research standard

What readers should expect.

What is a Market View?

A Market View connects a current economic or technical signal to the physical, organizational, and financial system beneath it.

Which sources does Engineer View use?

We prioritize government agencies, national laboratories, standards bodies, regulatory records, and original technical publications. Every quantitative claim links to its source.

How are facts separated from interpretation?

Source records establish observations. Engineer View labels scenarios, frameworks, and implications as analysis rather than presenting them as measured facts.

Does Engineer View provide investment or engineering advice?

No. The publication provides general research. Decisions require project specific technical, financial, legal, and regulatory review.

How are corrections handled?

Material source revisions or factual corrections are reflected in the research record. Publication dates and source dates remain visible.

Who publishes Engineer View?

Engineer View is an independent research publication of Skar Technologies, based in New York.
